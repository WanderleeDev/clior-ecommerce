## The four images

Every product gets the same four shots, named by slot:

| Slot | File | Shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `front` | `01-front.webp` | Packshot, straight-on, light neutral background |
| `angle` | `02-angle.webp` | Same product, three-quarter angle |
| `detail` | `03-detail.webp` | Macro of the material or texture |
| `lifestyle` | `04-lifestyle.webp` | Product in use, with a dog or cat in a home |

1024 × 1024, WebP. Files go to `data/images/products/<sku>/01-front.webp` and so on.
